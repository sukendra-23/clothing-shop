Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services.

What is your return policy?

We offer a complimentary 30-day return policy for all unworn items in their original condition with tags attached. Once we receive your return, a full refund will be issued to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship globally to over 50 countries. International shipping times vary between 3 to 10 business days depending on the destination. All applicable duties and taxes are calculated at checkout.

How can I track my order?

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive an email containing a tracking link. You can also track your order directly from your Cloth account dashboard under 'Order History'.

Are your products sustainable?

Sustainability is at the core of Cloth. We partner with ethical artisans and utilize eco-friendly materials whenever possible, aiming for a zero-carbon footprint by 2030.

How do I know my size?

We provide detailed size guides on every product page. Since we curate from various luxury brands, we recommend checking the specific measurements for each item. If you need personalized assistance, our style concierge is available via email.
